Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, username, phone number, course, year of study, university.
  • Profile Information: Skills, bio, AI experience level, interests.
  • Activity Data: Events registered, projects joined, resources accessed.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide club services and resources
  • To send you relevant updates and announcements
  • To improve the platform and user experience
  • To facilitate collaboration and networking
  • For research and statistical purposes (anonymized)

3. Data Security

  • Your data is encrypted and securely stored.
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.
  • Passwords are hashed using BCrypt.
  • We never share your information with third parties without your consent.

4. Cookies

  • Cookies are used only for session management.
  • We do not use tracking cookies.
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections to your data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

6. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • You can request data deletion at any time.
  • Anonymized data may be retained for research purposes.
